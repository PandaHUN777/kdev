# Code of Conduct

kdev follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere you represent the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and leave no room
for harassment, insults or personal attacks.

**Reporting.** If someone's behaviour concerns you, report it privately to the
maintainer, [@tushar-mahalya](https://github.com/tushar-mahalya), at the email
address on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block people, as the
Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
